## 🧠 Our Mission

TOAQ is dedicated to the rigorous study of cognitive sciences and their application to artificial intelligence. We explore how computational models can simulate cognitive processes and emotional-like states, always guided by a strong ethical framework. Our goal is to foster open, transparent, and interdisciplinary research.

This organization (`TOAQ-oss`) hosts the public repositories, datasets, and tools that support the TOAQ Ecosystem.

## 🔬 Core Initiatives

* **Cognitive Modeling:** Open-source architectures focusing on non-linear computational models and artificial cognition.
* **Audio & Voice Datasets:** Open vocal datasets designed to support research in human-machine interaction, speech analysis, and voice synthesis.
* **Research Broadcasting:** A 3-series podcast ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between complex academic research and the broader tech community.
* **Open Submissions:** A dedicated pipeline allowing researchers and peers to submit papers and collaborate on ethical AI frameworks.

## 🤝 Get Involved

We believe that advancing AI safety and cognitive modeling requires diverse perspectives from scientists, developers, and ethicists.

1. **Explore our code:** Review our [public repositories](https://github.com/orgs/TOAQ-oss/repositories).
2. **Experiment with our data:** Visit our [Hugging Face profile](https://huggingface.co/TOAQ) for open datasets and models.
3. **Submit your work:** We actively collaborate with peers. Learn how to use our submission system for your research papers on [toaq.fr](https://toaq.fr/).

*Please pay attention to the specific open-source licenses associated with each repository.*
